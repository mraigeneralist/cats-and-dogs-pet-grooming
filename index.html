Our Story

A Salon Born From
Pure Love for Pets 🐾

We started Cats & Dogs in 2003 with a simple belief: every pet deserves to be treated like family. Over two decades, we've groomed thousands of furry regulars right here in Anna Nagar, becoming a trusted name in Chennai's pet community.


Our team handles every animal with patience and calm — because a relaxed pet means a better groom, and a happier owner. We stay up-to-date with grooming techniques and only use quality, pet-safe products.


Whether it's your dog's first groom or your cat's monthly spa day — you'll see the difference.
